## Optional analytics

Google Analytics is off until you select **Accept analytics**. Rejecting analytics does not affect access to the website. Before you accept, this site does not load the Google Analytics script or send analytics events to Google.

If you accept, Google Analytics receives usage information such as pages viewed, browser and device information, and approximate location. Google also receives technical connection information when your browser contacts its service. The site disables Google signals and advertising personalization in its tag configuration. Initial page views omit URL query strings and fragments and include only the referring site's origin.

Analytics cookies beginning with `_ga` are configured to expire after 30 days without refreshing their expiry on each visit. Google's processing and retention of collected information are separate from the lifetime of cookies in your browser. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses information from sites using its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Your choice and browser storage

Use **Cookie settings** in the footer to accept or reject analytics at any time. Withdrawing consent stops future analytics collection on this site and removes accessible Google Analytics cookies. It does not automatically delete information already collected by Google.

You can close the notice without choosing. This hides it for the current tab using session storage, including as you browse other pages. Closing it does not grant consent or change an existing choice. You can reopen it through **Cookie settings**.

Your choice and the date you made it are saved in local browser storage for up to 180 days. This preference contains no visitor identifier and is not sent to an analytics service. After it expires, the site asks again. If browser storage is blocked, your choice applies only to the current page and the site may ask again when you navigate or reload.

## Hosting and other services

Cloudflare hosts and delivers this website. It receives information needed to deliver requests and operate its network, such as IP addresses and request details, independently of your analytics choice. See [Cloudflare's Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

The booking button uses Cal.com. Its embed script loads when you visit the site, and the booking interface loads when you open it. Information you submit when booking, such as your name and email address, is processed by Cal.com and shared with me to arrange the meeting. See [Cal.com's Privacy Policy](https://cal.com/privacy).

Some project pages embed YouTube videos using its privacy-enhanced domain or load GitHub star badges from Shields.io. Those services receive connection information when their content loads, independently of Google Analytics consent. External websites linked from this site have their own privacy practices. Fonts and the project images stored on this site are served from this site's hosting.
