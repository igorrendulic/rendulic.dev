I’m Igor Rendulic, a software and AI engineer who enjoys turning complex ideas into useful products. My work spans backend systems, data infrastructure, computer vision, and conversational AI—from the first prototype to the systems that keep a product running in production.

Over my career, I’ve co-founded a software company, led engineering teams, and stayed closely involved in designing and building software. I’ve worked on health and fitness platforms, food recognition, video streaming, and AI voice conversations. That breadth has shaped how I approach engineering: understand the problem, make thoughtful technical choices, and take responsibility for how the result works for the people using it.

I’m comfortable moving between product decisions, architecture, and code. I enjoy the early stages of figuring out what to build, as well as the practical work of making it reliable, responsive, and easier to maintain.

What keeps me interested is learning through building. I’m drawn to problems that bring different disciplines together and to working with people who care about the details. This site is where I share those projects, the decisions behind them, and what I learn along the way.
